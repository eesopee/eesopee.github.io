Biography

I am an observational researcher working at the intersection of galactic dynamics, gas kinematically resolved spectroscopy, and cosmic structure evolution. My research leverages multi-wavelength dataset arrays from space and ground observatories.

My academic journey began with a focus on physics and numerical astrophysics, followed by doctoral research investigating high-redshift galaxy kinematics and interstellar medium diagnostics.

Research Interests

Primary Themes

Galactic Kinematics

Investigating ionized gas rotation curves and galactic outflows in star-forming galaxies across cosmic time.

Interstellar Physics

Spectroscopic diagnostics of H II regions, dust grain temperatures, and interstellar extinction laws.

Astrostatistics

Automated spectral emission-line fitting pipelines using Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods.
